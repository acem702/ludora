Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Ludora is a Ludo game for Android and iOS, made by Suleiman Akaaba and Rasheedah Musa ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

The short version.
  • You can play without an account. Offline games stay on your phone.
  • An account is optional. It holds a username, a protected password, your profile and your stats, so they follow you to a new phone and put you on the leaderboard.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • The app asks for no permissions: no microphone, camera, location or contacts.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on this website.

1. Information kept on your phone

The game saves the following on your device only: your display name and avatar, a random player ID created on first launch, your settings, your match statistics, and an unfinished game so you can resume it. If you sign in, a session token is stored in your phone's secure storage. We can't see this information unless you play online or sign in, as described below. Uninstalling the app removes it.

2. Information our server handles

WhatWhenWhyHow long
Display name, avatar and random player ID When you play online To seat you in a room and show you to the other players For as long as the room exists. It is deleted when the room closes.
Game moves, strikes and results Online matches To run the match fairly on the server Until the room closes. If you are signed in, the result is added to your stats and rating.
Chat messages, emotes and quick phrases Online rooms. Typed messages are available only in private rooms; quick match offers emotes and set phrases only. To deliver them to the other players in the room Until the room closes. We don't keep chat history after that.
Username and password If you create an account To sign you in Until you delete the account. Passwords are stored only as a one-way scrypt hash, never in readable form.
Profile and stats (name, avatar, games played, wins, captures and similar) If you are signed in To sync them between your devices Until you delete the account
Skill rating and rated-game history Signed-in players in quick matches against other signed-in players For the leaderboard Until you delete the account
Session tokens Each sign-in To keep you signed in Until you sign out or delete the account. Tokens are stored only in hashed form.
IP address and basic request details (time, address requested) Every connection Security: rate limiting to stop password guessing, and diagnosing faults Rate-limit counters last minutes and are held in memory only. Web server logs are deleted after 14 days.

3. What other players can see

Players in your room see your display name, avatar, colour, moves and messages. The public leaderboard shows the display name, avatar, rating and number of rated games of signed-in players with enough rated games. Your username and password are never shown to anyone. Choose a display name that doesn't reveal personal details.

4. Why we may use this information

We use the information above only to provide the game you ask for (the contract between us), to keep the service secure and fair (our legitimate interests), and to meet legal obligations. We don't use it for advertising, profiling or automated decisions about you. The rating is a game score and has no effect outside the game.

5. Who we share it with

  • Hosting. Our server runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in a data centre in Phoenix, Arizona, United States. Oracle stores the data for us and may not use it for its own purposes.
  • App stores. Apple and Google distribute the app and handle downloads, under their own privacy policies. We receive only the anonymous, aggregated reports they give every developer.
  • The law. We will disclose information if a valid legal request requires it, or if it is needed to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. International transfers

Our server is in the United States, so information from online play is transferred there from wherever you are. We protect it in transit with encryption (HTTPS and secure WebSockets) and keep the amount we hold to the minimum the game needs.

7. Security

Connections to our server are encrypted. Passwords and session tokens are stored only as hashes, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and the server is reachable only through an encrypted gateway. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

8. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. Enter your password to confirm.
  • On the web: use the delete account page.
  • By email: write to akabasuleiman@gmail.com from any address, giving your username. We may ask you to confirm the request.

Deletion removes your account, profile, stats, rating, rating history and sessions from the live server straight away. Our daily backups are kept for 7 days, so the data is gone from them within 7 days too. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and, in Europe, the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to a data protection authority. In Nigeria, that authority is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. You can edit your name and avatar in the app at any time. For anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

10. Children

Accounts and online play are for people aged 13 or over. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also announce it in the app.
