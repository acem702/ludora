Questions people ask

Is Ludora free?

Yes. Ludora is free to download and free to play on Google Play, and it shows no ads.

Can I play Ludo offline?

Yes. Games against bots and pass-and-play games on one phone work offline. You only need a connection for online matches.

Do I need an account to play Ludora?

No account is needed to play offline. An account is only required for online play, and account holders must be 13 or older.

Does Ludora track me or show ads?

No. Ludora has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for marketing. The privacy policy lists everything the game stores.

How do I delete my Ludora account?

Use the delete account page. It signs in to the game server and permanently removes your account and the data stored with it.
