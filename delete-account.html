Delete your Ludora account

This permanently removes your account and everything stored with it on our server.

What gets deleted

  • Your username and password
  • Your profile: display name and avatar
  • Your synced stats, skill rating, rating history and leaderboard places
  • Every sign-in session, on every device

It's removed from the live server straight away and from our backups within 7 days. Nothing is kept. Stats saved on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app, and you can keep playing as a guest.

Option 1: in the app

Open Ludora, tap your profile at the top of the home screen, then Account → Delete account. Enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: here

Option 3: by email

Forgot your password? Email akabasuleiman@gmail.com with your username and the subject "Delete my Ludora account". We may ask you to confirm the request, and we will delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.
